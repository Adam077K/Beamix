## Pain Categories

### [Pain 1] — B2B SaaS: invisible in the research phase
*Source: Research-Lead async run 2026-05-23 — Q1 ICP/WTP research · Industry survey data (Gartner, McKinsey, Forrester)*

**Pain:** 73% of B2B buyers use AI search before a vendor demo. Only 14% of SaaS companies have a mature AI-visibility strategy. SaaS founders know AI search is important but don't know how to act on it — and can't afford to hire a specialist.

**Customer quote (paraphrase from research):** "I have no idea if ChatGPT mentions us when a buyer is researching our category." ★★★

**Frequency:** Consistent across B2B SaaS segment <$5M ARR
**JTBD:** "When I'm trying to grow pipeline, I want to show up when buyers are researching vendors, so I can be on their shortlist before they even visit my site."

---

### [Pain 2] — Legal: high CPL, referral channel is changing
*Source: Research-Lead async run 2026-05-23 — Q2 ICP vertical research · WordStream legal CPL data*

**Pain:** Legal digital CPL $649–$784 — highest of any industry. Law firms are accustomed to paying for referrals (referral networks, bar associations) but AI search is now part of how clients find lawyers, and most firms don't know how to appear in those answers.

**Customer quote (paraphrase):** "Someone Googled me and then asked ChatGPT and I wasn't in the answer. The competitor down the street was." ★★★

**Frequency:** Consistent — managing partners at solo/small firms are acutely aware of referral channel changes
**JTBD:** "When a prospective client is searching for a lawyer, I want my firm to come up in the AI answer, so I can capture consultation requests without paying a referral cut."

---

### [Pain 3] — Dental: already paying for local SEO, AI is next
*Source: Research-Lead async run 2026-05-23 — Q3 ICP vertical research · Industry survey data*

**Pain:** Dental practices already pay $800–$1,500/mo for local SEO. They understand the concept of paying for visibility. AI search is adding a new layer they don't know how to manage — and they don't have time to learn.

**Customer quote (paraphrase):** "I'm paying $1,000/month for SEO and I'm still not showing up when patients ask ChatGPT for a dentist in my area." ★★★

**Frequency:** Very consistent — single-location practices that already pay for local SEO
**JTBD:** "When a patient searches for a dentist in my city, I want to be in the AI answer, so I can get new patient bookings without adding another vendor to manage."
