## GTM cost model

**Zero paid acquisition through customer #50.** GTM cost is Adam's time only (not a cash expense for the business, but a real opportunity cost).

Estimated Adam time commitment (assumed):
- Discovery calls: 1hr each × 50 customers = 50 hours
- Brand brief reviews: 30 min each = 25 hours
- Total: ~75 hours through first 50 customers

At market rate for founder time ($150-300/hr), effective CAC for beachhead = $225-$450/customer (assumed opportunity cost, not cash).

**This is irrelevant to unit economics** until paid acquisition begins, but surfaces why Adam scales out to agent-led onboarding by customer #50: diminishing returns on founder time as primary acquisition channel.
