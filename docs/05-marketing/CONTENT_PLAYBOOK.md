## What this doc is

This is the operating doc for Beamix content production — blog, vertical guides, and the State of AI Search annual report. Content serves two purposes:

1. **SEO/GEO:** Rank on Google and get cited by LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) for vertical-specific AI search queries
2. **Sales enablement:** Content Adam sends after a cold DM, as context before a discovery call, and as proof during the call

Beamix sells GEO services, so our content must exemplify GEO. Every article we publish is a live demonstration of what we do for customers.

---

## The 10 rules (unchanged from v1.0 — still correct)

1. **One statistic in the first 100 words.** Sourced, linked. The Princeton GEO paper measured +41% citation lift from statistics addition. Do it.
2. **One direct quotation from a named source per article.** +28% citation lift. Not "experts say" — a real name.
3. **Inline citation links for every factual claim.** Not a "Sources" section at the bottom. Inline. With the URL and the year.
4. **FAQPage schema on every article that has a Q&A block.** 3.2× more likely to appear in Google AI Overviews.
5. **Real human author with Person schema.** 74% of sites hit by Google's Helpful Content Update lacked author bylines.
6. **Last-updated date visible at top of every article** — not just published date.
7. **Ban the AI tell-words.** No "delve," "tapestry," "realm," "leverage," "utilize," "moreover," "in conclusion," "it's important to note."
8. **One contrarian sentence per article.** "Most advice says X. That's wrong because Y."
9. **Specific numbers beat round numbers.** 37% not "about a third."
10. **First-hand experience signal in every article.** "We scanned 50 law firms and found..." "When a dental practice ran our process..."

## Per-article workflow

**Step 1 — Brief (CMO or Growth Lead, 30 min)**
- Title, target keyword, vertical, ICP, GEO angle, target word count, primary author
- One sentence: why this article exists — the decision it informs for a Beamix prospect

**Step 2 — Outline (Writer, 30 min)**
- H2/H3 structure
- For each section: which statistic, quote, example, and link goes in

**Step 3 — AI draft (Writer, 15 min)**
- Use the voice-priming prefix from HUMAN_SOUNDING_CONTENT.md §6
- First draft = raw material, not the ship product

**Step 4 — Banned-word sweep (Writer, 5 min)**
- Find/replace using CONTENT_STYLE_GUIDE.md §3

**Step 5 — Humanization edit (Editor, 30–45 min per 1,000 words)**
- Sentence-length variance
- 3+ specifics per 1,000 words
- One contrarian paragraph
- Strip hedges and corporate qualifiers
- Read aloud check

**Step 6 — E-E-A-T injection (Editor, 15 min)**
- Real author byline + Person schema link
- Last-updated date
- First-hand experience element
- 2+ outbound links to authoritative sources
- Internal links to ≥3 related Beamix articles

**Step 7 — Schema check (Editor, 10 min)**
- Article/BlogPosting schema
- FAQPage schema if Q&A block exists
- BreadcrumbList schema
- Person schema on author

**Step 8 — Detector sanity check (Editor, 5 min)**
- Run through Originality.ai — target <30% AI score

**Step 9 — Publish (CMO or Growth Lead, 15 min)**
- Framer Blog Collection entry
- Cover image (1200×630, <300KB)
- FAQ Items populated if applicable
- Submit to Search Console + Bing Webmaster (Bing powers ChatGPT)

**Step 10 — Distribute**
- LinkedIn post from Adam's account (not Beamix brand)
- Share in relevant vertical community thread when genuinely helpful
- Cold DM hook if the article directly addresses a prospect's situation

## Agency pivot: what changed in content strategy

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

**Before:** Content positioned Beamix as a tool. "Beamix runs agents that produce the deliverable."
**After:** Content positions Beamix as a done-for-you agency. "We produce and publish the deliverable on your behalf."

**Before:** Content addressed any SMB owner who wanted to fix AI visibility themselves.
**After:** Content addresses the 3 launch ICPs: B2B SaaS founders/VP Marketing, law firm managing partners, dental practice owners.

**Before:** CTAs drove to free scan → trial signup.
**After:** CTAs drive to free scan → discovery call booking. The call is the next step, not the checkout page.

**No agent names in content.** We do not mention "the FAQ agent," "the Schema agent," or any internal agent name. We describe what we produce: "we publish FAQ content," "we add schema markup," "we build citations."

## What NOT to do

- Publish without a real human author with Person schema
- Publish anything containing a banned word
- Write generic "What is GEO?" articles — vertical-specific is the only format that works for our ICPs
- Name internal agents in customer-facing content
- Frame content as "here's how to DIY this" — we are an agency, not a tool tutorial
- Round numbers when you have the real ones
- Publish placeholder testimonials
