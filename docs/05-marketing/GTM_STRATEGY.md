# Beamix — GTM Strategy

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*
*v2.0 · Owner: CMO*

---

## Beachhead motion: customers 1–50

Zero paid acquisition until customer #50 case studies exist. Every dollar spent on paid before then is a dollar spent without social proof. Social proof in an agency sale is not optional.

### Customers 1–10: warm network + cold DMs

Adam personally contacts 50 named businesses per vertical (150 total). LinkedIn + warm Israeli SMB intros + direct cold DMs referencing their actual website scan results.

DM format: founder-to-founder, ~100 words, references specific scan finding ("I ran your site through our scan, here's what's broken"), low-pressure CTA to a 30-minute discovery call.

Templates at `docs/05-marketing/handoff/discovery-dm-templates.md`.

### Customers 11–20: "State of AI Search" report

The annual "State of AI Search" editorial report drops at MVP+90. This is a data report on AI search visibility across Israeli and global SMBs — original research, not opinion. It becomes the content hook for outbound:

- "We published the State of AI Search report. Here's what we found about [vertical]."
- Report link goes in LinkedIn posts, cold emails, and community threads.
- 3 vertical blog posts per week launch simultaneously (one per launch ICP).

### Customers 21–30: LinkedIn + community engagement

- Adam's LinkedIn profile is the primary owned channel (not the Beamix brand page)
- Free scan link in Adam's LinkedIn bio and posts
- Vertical community engagement: Dentaltown, Above the Law/Avvo, IndieHackers/SaaStr
- Posts frame Adam as a practitioner, not a vendor: "I scanned 50 law firms. Here's what I found."

### Customers 31–50: referral incentive

$500 referral credit for any customer who refers a paying customer and agrees to be a case study.

Case study format: before/after AI visibility score, specific deliverables produced, time to first result.

---

## Channel hierarchy

1. **Warm network (primary)** — Adam's direct relationships in Israeli SMB community
2. **Cold DM outbound** — founder-to-founder, personalized to their scan result
3. **Content** — vertical blog posts + State of AI Search report as credibility anchor
4. **Community** — Dentaltown, Above the Law, IndieHackers, SaaStr (no spam, genuine contribution)
5. **Referral** — $500 credit per referred paying customer

No paid acquisition until customer #50.

## Discovery call structure

1. Run their free scan live (or share the pre-run result)
2. Walk through the AI visibility score per engine
3. Show what competitors appear for their top 3 queries
4. Explain the 60-day guarantee
5. Recommend the right tier based on their location count, industry, and budget
6. Send the onboarding link

Adam handles all discovery calls through customer #50. After that, the Discovery agent handles qualification and routing.

## What NOT to do

- Run paid ads before customer #50 case studies
- Pitch as a "tool" or "platform" — pitch as a done-for-you service
- Sell to a vertical outside the 3 launch ICPs (HVAC, real estate, DTC deferred to MVP+90)
- Promise specific ranking outcomes — promise to move AI visibility or refund
