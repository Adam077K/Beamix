## Value proposition — 3 sections

### Section 1: The referral channel is shifting

Legal digital CPL runs $649–$784 — the highest of any industry. Every new client acquisition matters.

AI search is now part of how prospective clients find lawyers. When someone needs a lawyer in Tel Aviv, they don't only ask colleagues. They ask ChatGPT. They ask Perplexity. And the firms that come up in those answers are getting consultation requests from clients who have already pre-qualified themselves based on the AI recommendation.

These are referral-quality inquiries — clients who arrived because the AI told them you were the right firm for their matter. No referral fee. No ad spend.

The firms showing up in those answers are not necessarily the biggest or the best-known. They're the ones whose digital presence is structured in the way AI engines read and cite.

**What Beamix does:** We build that structure for you. We scan every major AI engine for your practice area and location queries, identify exactly where you're invisible and why, then publish schema markup, FAQ content, entity citations, and attorney profile updates on your behalf. In the first 60 days, a typical legal engagement includes:

- **FAQ content** — answers to the questions prospective clients ask AI engines before their first call ("what are the best employment lawyers in [city]?", "what does a non-compete dispute cost?", "how do I find a wrongful dismissal lawyer?")
- **Entity citation building** — your firm listed accurately on the platforms AI engines reference for legal services (Avvo, FindLaw, Martindale-Hubbell, law society directories, Google My Business)
- **Practice area page optimization** — GEO-tuned updates to your service pages, written in the language prospective clients use when querying AI engines
- **Weekly digest** — what we did, what moved, what's in the approval queue

We don't give you a report and leave the fixing to you. We do the fixing.

### Section 3: Why YMYL matters for law firms — and why it's an advantage

AI engines apply higher trust standards to legal content (YMYL — Your Money, Your Life). This means generic firms with weak digital presence lose ground faster than in other categories. But it also means that firms with strong entity authority, verified citations, and structured content gain disproportionate visibility.

Your credentials, your bar admission, your case outcomes — these are the signals that create trust for AI engines. We surface them in the right format.

A solo firm with structured digital presence can appear above a 50-partner firm with none. That's not an accident. It's the signal system.
