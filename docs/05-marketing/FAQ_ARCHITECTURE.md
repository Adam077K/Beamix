## Design principles (unchanged)

Every answer in this document is written to satisfy two requirements simultaneously:

1. **FAQPage JSON-LD extraction** — each answer is self-contained, starts with a definition or direct statement, and is 40–80 words. Google reads these for AI Overviews.
2. **LLM passage extraction** — definition-first structure, one fact per sentence, no hedging, no "it depends." Perplexity and ChatGPT lift passages that can stand alone as answers.

Answers are intentionally short and dense. Long-form explanation belongs in the linked blog article.

**Q9: What is Beamix?**

Beamix is a done-for-you GEO agency for B2B SaaS companies, law firms, and dental practices. We get your business cited in AI search results — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews — by publishing schema markup, citations, FAQ content, and GEO-tuned pages on your behalf. We do the work. You approve key actions and see the results in your outcomes dashboard.

---

**Q10: How is Beamix different from a tool like Profound or Otterly?**

Profound and Otterly show you data — where you rank, which competitors appear, what AI engines say. Beamix does the fixing. After your free scan, we run a discovery call to understand your business, then we publish schema, citations, FAQ content, and listings on your behalf. You get outcomes, not reports. For B2B SaaS founders, law firms, and dental practices that don't have time to act on data separately, Beamix collapses measurement and delivery into one service.

**Q11: What about my existing SEO agency?**

Beamix is GEO-only — we focus exclusively on AI search visibility (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Traditional SEO agencies optimize for Google page 1 rankings. The signals AI engines use are different from traditional SEO signals — structured schema markup, FAQ content, citation authority, and entity consistency. Most SEO agencies are not yet optimizing for these. Beamix and your existing SEO agency can work in parallel; the work we produce (schema, FAQ content, citations) complements traditional SEO.

---

**Q12: What happens in the onboarding call?**

The onboarding call (called the brand fingerprint session) is typically 45–60 minutes. We review your free scan results together, identify the 3 most impactful gaps, collect information about your services, target locations, and key customer questions, and walk you through the approval gate process. By the end of the call, we have everything we need to begin work. The first deliverables are typically ready within 48 hours.

---

**Q13: What does Beamix actually produce — what do I receive?**

Depending on your tier and the gaps identified in your scan, Beamix produces: schema markup files (added via your CMS or GTM), FAQ content optimized for AI engine extraction, citations and directory listings on major platforms, GEO-tuned content pages, Google Business Profile updates, and (on Growth and above) GEO-optimized outreach emails. Every action is logged in your outcomes dashboard. You approve content publishing actions before they go live — schema and citations are automatic on stable platforms.

---

**Q14: How does the approval gate work?**

Beamix uses a tiered approval model. Schema markup, citation listings, directory updates, and AI-engine crawl notifications are automatic — you don't need to approve every schema file. Content publishing (blog posts, FAQ pages, emails sent on your behalf) requires one-click approval from your weekly digest. You review a summary of what we're about to publish, click approve or request changes, and we proceed. Nothing goes live without your sign-off on content.

---

**Q15: Do I need a website to use Beamix?**

Yes. Beamix optimizes web-based content and schema markup. You need a website to benefit from the schema, FAQ content, and GEO-tuned page work we produce. If your site is on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, Squarespace, or Wix, we can publish directly or provide paste-ready instructions. Custom CMS sites are supported on the Professional tier.

---

**Q16: What about my Wix or Squarespace site?**

Wix and Squarespace have limited API access for automated publishing, but we support them with a paste-ready workflow. We produce the content and schema markup, then send you 1-click instructions showing exactly where to paste each piece. Most tasks take under 5 minutes for you to complete. Direct auto-publish is available on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Apple Maps, and schema via Google Tag Manager.

---

**Q17: What is the outcomes dashboard?**

Your outcomes dashboard shows your AI visibility score per engine (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), your score trend over time, weekly wins ("cited for this query for the first time"), the approval queue for upcoming content publishes, and a full drill-down trail showing exactly what we did to produce each result. You can see the connection between every action we took and every score change.

---

**Q18: How does Beamix measure results?**

We measure AI visibility the same way we produce it: by querying each AI engine with real prompts about your industry and location — the same queries your customers type. Your visibility score is a 0–100 number weighted by query volume and engine reach. It updates with every scan cycle (weekly on Starter, twice-weekly on Growth, daily on Scale). You can see the before-and-after impact of every deliverable we publish.

---

**Q19: What is the 60-day guarantee?**

If we don't move your AI search visibility in 60 days, you don't pay. No questions, no phone tree, no contract. Cancel in one click and keep everything we produced. To qualify, you must complete the 3-step activation: onboarding call + property connect + first scan. This ensures we actually have access to do the work before the clock starts.

**Q20: What does "move your AI search visibility" mean specifically?**

It means your AI visibility score across our tracked engines is measurably higher at day 61 than it was at day 1. We track your score at the start of your engagement, publish deliverables according to your tier's schedule, and compare scores at day 61. If the score hasn't moved, you get a full refund. We define "moved" as a statistically meaningful improvement — not a rounding error. The measurement is objective and based on live AI engine queries, not our own modeling.

## Hub 5 — Technical FAQ

(Q35–Q40 are retained from v1.0 — technical GEO fundamentals are unchanged by the agency pivot, except Q37 which is updated for agency publishing model.)

---

**Q35: What is FAQPage schema and why does it matter for AI search?** *(retained)*

FAQPage schema is a structured data format (JSON-LD) that explicitly marks up question-and-answer content on a web page so search engines and AI engines can extract it cleanly. Pages with FAQPage schema are 3.2× more likely to appear in Google AI Overviews than pages without it. We add FAQPage schema as part of every Beamix engagement.

---

**Q36: How does Beamix scan AI search engines?** *(retained)*

Beamix queries each AI engine with real prompts constructed from your business's industry, location, and service keywords — the same queries your customers type. Results are recorded and scored: whether your business is mentioned, at what position, with what sentiment. Scans run on a schedule (weekly on Starter, twice-weekly on Growth, daily on Scale) and are triggered after every major publishing batch.

---

**Q37: How does Beamix publish content to my website?**

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

Publishing method depends on your CMS. For WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Apple Maps, and schema via Google Tag Manager — we publish directly using API access you grant during onboarding. For Squarespace and Wix — we send paste-ready instructions with exact placement guidance. Custom CMS platforms are supported on the Professional tier via direct API integration. Nothing is published without your approval on content actions.

---

**Q38: What is llms.txt?** *(retained — still accurate)*

llms.txt is a proposed plain-text file at your site root that describes your site's content for AI engines. We implement it as part of standard onboarding — it's low cost and signals best-practice intent, though no major AI platform has confirmed they read it. Custom robots.txt configuration (allowing AI search crawlers) matters significantly more, and we handle that during onboarding.

---

**Q39: Does Beamix manage Google Business Profile?** *(updated)*

Yes, on Scale and Professional tiers. Beamix manages GBP updates — services, photos, posts, and Q&A — as part of the local AI visibility work. Starter and Growth tiers receive GBP optimization recommendations; direct management requires Scale or above.

---

**Q40: Is my scan data private?** *(retained)*

Scan data in Beamix is private to your account. We don't share individual business results with other users or publish them externally. We may use aggregated, anonymized data in our State of AI Search reports — but no individual business is identifiable in that reporting. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
