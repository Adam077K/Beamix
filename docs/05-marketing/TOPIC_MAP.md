## Strategic rationale

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

Beamix has pivoted from a self-serve tool to a done-for-you GEO agency. The topic map reflects this: instead of broad SMB owner education (which suited a self-serve product), content is now vertical-specific and targets buyers who are looking for a service.

The 3 launch ICPs are: B2B SaaS, solo/small law firms, single-location dental.

Secondary differentiation: Hebrew. Zero GEO content vendor publishes in Hebrew. Even 3–5 strong Hebrew articles makes Beamix the default category owner in Israel.

## Publishing cadence

**Phase 1 (customers 1–10): Pillar pages first**
- Build the 4 pillar pages before publishing cluster articles
- Rationale: pillars create the internal link structure; cluster articles link back to them

**Phase 2 (customers 11–20): 3 cluster articles/week**
- One per vertical, aligned with State of AI Search report launch
- "State of AI Search" report ships in this phase (MVP+90)

**Phase 3 (customers 21–50): Guarantee cluster + data reports**
- 60-day guarantee cluster articles publish as case study data accumulates
- Vertical-specific data reports publish quarterly
