# Beamix — Channel Mix

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*
*v2.0 · Owner: CMO*

---

## Channel philosophy

Every channel before customer #50 exists to generate discovery calls. Not awareness. Not impressions. Discovery calls.

No paid channel until customer #50 has signed and delivered a case study.

---

## Primary channels

### LinkedIn (Adam's personal profile)

The highest-leverage channel at this stage. Founder-led, practitioner voice.

**Content types:**
- "I scanned 50 [vertical] businesses. Here's what I found." — data post with free scan hook
- "Someone asked ChatGPT for [query]. Here's who came up." — awareness post for target vertical
- "We moved a law firm's AI visibility score from 23 to 71 in 60 days. Here's what we did." — case study when available

**Targeting:** B2B SaaS founders and VP Marketing, managing partners at law firms, dental practice owners. Connect + message (not InMail) after commenting on their content.

**Cadence:** 3 posts/week minimum once content motion starts (customers 21–30 milestone).

---

### Warm Israeli SMB network

Adam's direct relationships in the Israeli business community. This is the fastest path to customers 1–10.

**Approach:** Personal intro, not sales email. "I built something and I want to test it with 10 businesses I know. It's free for the first scan and the discovery call costs you 30 minutes."

**Referral path:** After customer #10, ask each paying customer for one intro to someone in their network.

---

### Cold DMs (founder-to-founder)

**Platform:** LinkedIn primarily. WhatsApp for warm intros.

**Format:** 80–150 words. References their specific website and scan result. Low-pressure CTA to a 30-minute discovery slot.

**Volume:** 50 named businesses per vertical, per week, during the customers 1–10 phase.

Templates at `docs/05-marketing/handoff/discovery-dm-templates.md`.

## Secondary channels (activate at customers 21–30)

### Content (blog + State of AI Search)

3 vertical blog posts per week. One per launch ICP. Published on the Framer marketing site.

Each post follows CONTENT_PLAYBOOK.md requirements: real author, sourced statistics, FAQPage schema, no AI tells.

The "State of AI Search" annual report (MVP+90) is the flagship content asset — a data report on AI visibility across Israeli and global SMBs. It functions as the primary outbound hook for customers 11–20.

### Email (discovery call follow-up)

No cold email blast. Email is for follow-up after a LinkedIn connection or warm intro. One email, one CTA (book a call), no nurture sequence until customer #50.

## Paid channels (deferred)
