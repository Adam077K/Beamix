# Beamix — Content Style Guide

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*
*v2.0 · Owner: CMO*

---

## Who this guide is for

Every person or tool that produces content for Beamix: blog writers, editors, AI writing tools, social media, email, and ad copy. If words go out with the Beamix name on them, this guide applies.

When in doubt: read the sentence aloud. If it sounds like a press release, rewrite it. If it sounds like something a direct, knowledgeable colleague would say, publish it.

---

## 1. Beamix voice — three words, concrete definitions

### Authoritative

We know this material. We have scanned hundreds of SMB businesses across three verticals. We state things directly and don't hedge when we have evidence.

**Sounds like:** "73% of your buyers used AI before their last vendor demo. Beamix gets your product in those answers."

**Does not sound like:** "It may be beneficial to consider AI search visibility, as research suggests it could potentially improve discovery."

### Direct

We get to the point in the first sentence. We don't warm up, we don't contextualize. We say the thing.

**Sounds like:** "Your law firm is invisible to ChatGPT. Here's why and what we do about it."

**Does not sound like:** "In today's evolving digital landscape, AI search has become increasingly important for businesses of all sizes."

### Warm

We are talking to a real person who runs a real business and has real pressures. We use "you" and "your business." We're on their side. We don't lecture.

**Sounds like:** "If you run a 3-person employment law firm in Tel Aviv, this part matters for you specifically."

**Does not sound like:** "Business owners must take proactive steps to ensure optimal visibility."

## 2. Agency framing rules — critical update

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

Beamix is a done-for-you GEO agency. The language must reflect this at every point.

| Say this | Not this |
|----------|----------|
| "We publish schema markup on your behalf" | "The Schema agent generates a JSON-LD file" |
| "We build citations across AI engine corpora" | "The Citation agent runs outreach" |
| "We do the work" | "Our platform does the work" |
| "Beamix gets you cited in ChatGPT" | "Beamix enables you to get cited in ChatGPT" |
| "We track your AI visibility score weekly" | "The dashboard shows your weekly score" |
| "You approve. We publish." | "You review, then the agent publishes" |
| "What we produce for your business" | "What the agents produce" |

**No agent names in customer-facing copy.** Internal agent names (Discovery agent, Brand-brief manager, Approval-gate writer, etc.) are product implementation details. Customers see outcomes and deliverables — not the workforce.

| Principle | DO write this | DO NOT write this |

## 5. Sentence structure rules

### Length variance — mandatory

Every paragraph must have sentence-length variety. If every sentence is 18–22 words, the paragraph reads as machine-generated.

**Target mix:** At least one sentence under 8 words, at least one over 25 words, in every 4-sentence block.

### Use sentence fragments deliberately

Fragments add rhythm and emphasis. Use 1–2 per article.

Good: "Most GEO advice is wrong for SMBs. Not wrong in theory. Wrong for the actual problem."

Good: "Not a dashboard. A service with a guarantee."

### One-line paragraphs

Use for maximum emphasis. Max 2 per article.

### Don't open with subordinate clauses

Wrong: "When considering the importance of AI search visibility for law firms, it's clear that..."
Right: "Law firms are losing clients to AI search. Most don't know it yet."

## 6. GEO formatting conventions

These are structural requirements for AI citation lift, not preferences.

### TL;DR placement

Every article over 1,500 words gets a TL;DR block at the top. Format:

```
**TL;DR:** [One sentence summary. State the key number or takeaway.]
```

The TL;DR is the most-extracted passage by AI engines. Make it self-contained and quotable.

### Stat density

Minimum 1 sourced statistic per 300 words. Exact sourced numbers with citation link.

Format: "A [date] study by [Author/Source] found that [specific number + specific outcome]."

### Quotation usage

Every article over 1,000 words includes at least one direct quotation from a named source. A real person, real name.

Format: `"[Exact quote]" — [Full Name], [Title/Credential], [Year]`

### "Last updated" timestamp

Every article shows both publication date AND last-updated date at the top.

Format: `By [Author Name], [Credential] · Published [YYYY-MM-DD] · Last updated [YYYY-MM-DD] · [N min read]`

### Definition block

Every article includes a clear, self-contained definition of its core concept in the first 400 words. Format it as a standalone paragraph. This is the passage LLMs extract.

## 7. Vertical-specific voice adaptations

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

### B2B SaaS (founder + VP Marketing voice)

- Frame in terms of pipeline and buyer research stages, not "traffic" or "impressions"
- Numbers they care about: demo requests, trial signups, qualified pipeline
- Their vocabulary: "ICP," "pipeline," "demo," "category," "PLG," "ARR"
- Opening hook: "73% of your buyers researched with AI before their last vendor demo."
- Tone: peer-to-peer, data-forward. They are skeptical of marketing claims. Lead with research.

### Solo and small law firms (managing partner voice)

- Use precise language (not dumbed-down). Partners read this.
- Frame in terms of new matter intake and consultation bookings, not "leads"
- Numbers they care about: consultation requests, new matters, CPL
- Their vocabulary: "matter," "client intake," "referral," "consultation"
- Opening hook: a missed referral — "Someone asked ChatGPT for a lawyer in Tel Aviv. Here's who came up."

### Single-location dental (owner-dentist voice)

- Frame in terms of new patient appointments, not "leads" or "traffic"
- YMYL-adjacent — be precise, cite sources, avoid health claims
- Numbers they care about: new patients per month, appointment conversion rate
- Their vocabulary: "new patients," "scheduling," "insurance accepted," "specialty"
- Opening hook: a patient's search journey — "Patients are asking ChatGPT which dentist to call."

## 8. Hebrew voice note

For Hebrew content — this is not a translation guide. It is a tone brief.

Hebrew Beamix voice: **ישיר, מקצועי, ישראלי.** (Direct, professional, Israeli.)

- No formal academic Hebrew — business casual register (דיבורי עסקי)
- Israeli business owners are direct. Don't soften.
- Use Hebrew city names naturally (תל אביב, ירושלים, חיפה)
- The word "לבסוף" is "in conclusion" in Hebrew — avoid it
- The phrase "חשוב לציין" is "it's important to note" — avoid it
- Numbers stay in numerals: 41%, 3.2×, $499
- Hebrew content is written fresh in Hebrew with Israeli examples — not translated from English

## 9. No AI disclosure rule

*Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot*

Content produced by Beamix — whether for customers or for our own marketing — must read as human-written. No labels, no disclosure.

Do NOT add:
- "AI-generated"
- "Created by AI"
- "Powered by Claude"
- Any disclosure label on content items

Adam handles AI disclosure decisions separately. This rule applies to all customer-facing content without exception.

## 10. Pre-publish reviewer checklist

### E-E-A-T signals
- [ ] Real human byline with credential
- [ ] Author links to author archive page
- [ ] Author archive page has Person schema with sameAs links
- [ ] Last-updated date visible at top of article
- [ ] First-hand experience signal present ("We scanned 50 law firms and found...")
- [ ] At least 2 outbound links to authoritative sources
- [ ] At least 1 original data point from Beamix scanning

### GEO structure
- [ ] TL;DR block at top (articles 1,500w+)
- [ ] At least 1 sourced statistic in first 100 words
- [ ] At least 1 direct named quotation
- [ ] Self-contained definition block in first 400 words
- [ ] Sourced stat per 300 words throughout
- [ ] Internal links to ≥3 related Beamix articles
- [ ] CTA to free scan or discovery call booking

### Agency framing check
- [ ] No agent names appear in copy
- [ ] "We" is used for all actions Beamix takes
- [ ] "Platform" and "tool" language is replaced with agency framing
- [ ] No "AI-generated," "AI-powered," or disclosure language appears

### Schema
- [ ] Article/BlogPosting schema validates
- [ ] FAQPage schema present if article has Q&A block
- [ ] BreadcrumbList schema present

### Banned words
- [ ] Document searched for every word on §4 list
- [ ] No banned word remains in published version
- [ ] Read aloud check passes

### Meta / SEO
- [ ] Title tag: 50–60 characters, primary keyword included
- [ ] Meta description: 140–160 characters, benefit statement
- [ ] OG image: 1200×630px, under 300KB
