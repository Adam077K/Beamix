## Launch ICP — 3 verticals (locked 2026-05-23)

### Vertical 1: B2B SaaS companies < $5M ARR

**Profile:**
- Stage: seed to Series A, 5-50 employees
- Marketing budget: $3K-$20K/month
- AI-search behavior: 73% of SaaS buyers now use AI search as a primary research tool; 51% start their vendor search in an AI chatbot vs Google (fact — research run tasks/a98bc6df7d83e15e2, 2026-05-23)
- AI-visibility gap: only 14% of SaaS companies < $5M ARR have a mature AI-visibility strategy (fact — research run tasks/a98bc6df7d83e15e2, 2026-05-23)

**Why they buy:** Their buyer already lives in AI chat. Beamix gets them visible where buyers look. Beamix pricing ($499-$1,499) is line-item approved by VP Marketing without board review.

**Primary tier:** Growth ($999) or Scale ($1,499) — multi-location or global presence common even at seed stage.

**Discovery script angle:** "Your buyers are asking ChatGPT which [category] tool to use. Are you showing up?"

---

### Vertical 2: Solo + small law firms (1-5 attorneys)

**Profile:**
- Practice types: immigration, family law, estate planning, personal injury, employment
- Digital CPL: $649-$784/lead (highest of any industry) (fact — research run tasks/a98bc6df7d83e15e2, 2026-05-23)
- Monthly marketing budget: $1,000-$3,000/month for established firms
- AI-search relevance: YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) trust premium — AI engines heavily cite authoritative sources for legal queries

**Why they buy:** One additional AI-search-visible client per month pays for 3-6 months of Beamix. The ROI math is immediate and concrete. Attorneys understand retainer models — a monthly subscription is familiar.

**Primary tier:** Starter ($499) or Growth ($999) — single location, 1-3 practice areas.

**Discovery script angle:** "When someone asks ChatGPT 'best immigration lawyer in [city]', are you mentioned? Your CPL is $700 — if we get you 2 AI-search leads per month, Beamix pays for itself in the first week."

---

### Vertical 3: Single-location dental practices

**Profile:**
- Revenue: $500K-$2M/year per practice
- Established SEO spend: $800-$1,500/month for local SEO already in budget (fact — research run tasks/a98bc6df7d83e15e2, 2026-05-23)
- AI-search driver: "best dentist near me" queries increasingly go to AI before Google Maps
- Decision maker: practice owner or office manager; not a technical buyer

**Why they buy:** Already paying for local SEO; Beamix is the AI-era upgrade. Price ($499-$999) slots into existing agency budget. "Done for you" resonates — they have no time to run tools.

**Primary tier:** Starter ($499) — single location, local-only targeting.

**Discovery script angle:** "Your local SEO agency gets you on Google. But patients are now asking ChatGPT 'best dentist near [neighborhood]'. We handle the AI side — done for you, same price as your current local SEO."

### SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

Constraints: English + Hebrew only at launch; requires website (no pure offline businesses); requires some existing digital presence for scan to return data.

- Language filter: ~60% of target businesses operate in English or Hebrew at sufficient digital maturity (assumed)
- Website + digital presence filter: ~70% of target businesses have indexable web presence (assumed)
- **SAM: $1.2B-$2.6B × 0.60 × 0.70 = $504M-$1.09B ARR** (assumed)

## Customer persona updates (post-pivot)

**Old persona (self-serve):** Technical marketing manager who wants to learn a tool and track metrics.

**New persona (agency):** Business owner or practice manager who is time-poor, result-focused, and budget-approved for a $500-$1,500/mo professional service. They do not want to learn a tool. They want someone to handle AI visibility on their behalf and send them a weekly update.

**Key behavioral differences:**
- Does not want a dashboard (wants a digest)
- Does not want to configure agents (wants deliverables to show up)
- Does want proof of progress (weekly wins, visibility score movement)
- Does want approval control for content (but async, not synchronous)
