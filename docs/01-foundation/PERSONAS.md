### Persona 1 — Sam, B2B SaaS founder

- **Title:** Founder / VP Marketing
- **Company:** B2B SaaS, $1M–$5M ARR, 10–40 people
- **Location:** US, Israel, UK (English-speaking)
- **Budget:** $500–$3,000/mo for marketing tools; can approve $999–$1,499 if ROI is plausible
- **Why now:** 73% of buyers use AI in evaluation; 51% start in AI chatbot vs Google; only 14% have a mature AI-visibility strategy → enormous gap
- **Pain language:** "We're invisible in ChatGPT for our category." "Our competitors are getting mentioned and we aren't." "I don't know how to fix it and I can't afford an agency."
- **Job to be done:** "Make sure my SaaS shows up when buyers ask AI 'what's the best [category] tool?'"
- **Approval style:** Wants approval cards for content (marketing voice matters) and outreach. Auto on schema/citations/listings is fine.
- **Best-fit tier:** Growth $999/mo (multi-page sites — product / pricing / docs / blog / changelog)
- **Beachhead motion:** Adam personal LinkedIn + direct DMs to 50 named SaaS companies + warm intros

### Persona 2 — Dana, Solo / Small-Firm Lawyer

- **Title:** Solo attorney OR managing partner of a 2–5-lawyer firm
- **Practice areas:** Personal injury, criminal defense, family law, employment, immigration — practice-area pages each track separately
- **Location:** US (highest CPL legal market), Israel
- **Budget:** $1,000–$3,000/mo for marketing already (legal CPL $649–$784 is highest in any industry)
- **Why now:** YMYL trust premium → AI-mention drives qualified intake; legal directories (Avvo, Justia, FindLaw) are exactly the sources LLMs cite
- **Pain language:** "ChatGPT keeps recommending the same big firms — I'm not in the answer." "I pay $700+/lead on Google Ads and need a cheaper channel." "Ethics rules mean I have to approve every word that goes out as me."
- **Job to be done:** "Be the lawyer LLMs name when someone asks for representation in my practice area + city."
- **Approval style:** **Mandatory human approve on every content / email / outreach.** YMYL stays human-approve regardless of preference settings. Auto on schema/citations/listings only.
- **Best-fit tier:** Scale $1,499/mo (practice-area pages + multi-page coverage + ethics-compliant outreach)
- **Beachhead motion:** Warm intros via Israeli bar contacts + direct DMs to 50 US solo / small-firm law firms in PI / DUI / family law

### Persona 3 — Eli, Owner-Dentist (Single-Location Practice)

- **Title:** Owner-dentist
- **Practice:** Single-location general practice or specialty (pediatric, cosmetic, orthodontics)
- **Location:** US (highest spend, established habits), Israel
- **Budget:** $800–$1,500/mo currently spent on local SEO (NextDoorDental, ProSites, PatientPop) — pattern is established
- **Why now:** GBP + listing + review workflow already understood; AI-search overlay is incremental, not a new behavior. Patients increasingly ask "dentist near me that takes [insurance]" on AI.
- **Pain language:** "I already pay for local SEO — I need this to be additive, not replace it." "I don't log in to dashboards." "Just tell me what you did each week."
- **Job to be done:** "Show up when someone asks Gemini/ChatGPT for the best dentist near them."
- **Approval style:** Weekly digest by email → approves via mobile in 5 minutes. Auto on schema/citations/listings + 1-click on FAQ content. Doesn't want phone calls.
- **Best-fit tier:** Starter $499/mo entry, upgrades to Growth $999/mo when adding second location
- **Beachhead motion:** Warm intros via Israeli dental community + direct DMs to 50 US single-location dental practices
