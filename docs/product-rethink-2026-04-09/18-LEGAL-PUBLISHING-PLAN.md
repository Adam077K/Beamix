## Terms of Service — required clauses

Draft via `legal-advisor` skill + Termly's SaaS T&Cs template, customized for:

1. **Service description.** Beamix is a GEO platform — scans AI engines for visibility, runs agents to produce content drafts, never auto-publishes. User publishes everything manually. (Limits Beamix's liability for what gets published.)
2. **Acceptable use.** No spam, no scraping, no automated abuse of the free scan, no using outputs to deceive AI engines via cloaking.
3. **YMYL exclusion.** Beamix does not serve legal-advisory, medical-diagnostic, financial-advisory, or regulated Israeli professions. See `04-EMPTY-STATES.md` §Excluded industry.
4. **Refund policy.** 14-day money-back guarantee, with run-aware deduction per ADQ-5 (cap refund to 50% if >50% of runs consumed). Top-up purchases ($19/10 runs) are non-refundable once any run is consumed.
5. **Subscription cancellation.** Cancel any time via Settings → Billing → Paddle portal. Access continues until end of paid period.
6. **AI content disclaimer.** "Outputs are AI-generated drafts. User is responsible for fact-checking, verifying claims, and complying with platform rules (Google E-E-A-T, EU AI Act Article 50, etc.) before publishing." (Per memory `feedback_no_ai_labels.md` — Beamix does NOT auto-disclose AI authorship; user owns disclosure.)
7. **Limitation of liability.** Cap at trailing-12-month subscription fees paid. Standard SaaS boilerplate.
8. **Governing law.** Israel jurisdiction (Adam's domicile). Arbitration clause optional — `legal-advisor` to recommend.
9. **Termination.** Beamix can suspend for breach (spam, abuse, non-payment after Paddle dunning exhausts). User can terminate anytime via cancel.
10. **Service availability.** No SLA for MVP. Best-effort uptime. (Add explicit SLA tier in Scale tier post-launch if customers ask.)

## Privacy Policy — GDPR-grade required clauses

1. **Data controller.** Beamix (Adam's registered entity).
2. **Data we collect.** Email, business name, scan URL, scan results, agent outputs, payment info (via Paddle — Beamix doesn't store card data), browser fingerprint (PostHog), IP for security.
3. **Why we collect it.** Service delivery, billing, product improvement, security.
4. **Legal basis (GDPR Article 6).** Contract (subscription), legitimate interest (security + analytics), consent (cookies, marketing email).
5. **Data retention windows** (mirrors `21-DATA-GOVERNANCE.md`):
   - Scans / free_scans: 90 days post-cancellation
   - agent_jobs: 90 days post-cancellation
   - inbox_items / archive_items: indefinite (user-owned content assets)
   - audit_log: 7 years (compliance)
   - llm_cost_events: 13 months
   - Billing records: 7 years (Israeli tax law)
6. **Subprocessors.** Public list — kept current:
   - Supabase (Frankfurt — DB + Auth + Storage)
   - Paddle (merchant-of-record, payment processing)
   - Inngest (job orchestration)
   - OpenRouter (LLM routing — Gemini, GPT, Perplexity scan engines)
   - Anthropic (direct Claude API — primary agent runtime)
   - Perplexity (Sonar API — citation verification)
   - Resend (transactional email via `notify.beamixai.com`)
   - PostHog (EU — product analytics)
   - Sentry (error monitoring)
   - Vercel (hosting + CDN)
7. **Cross-border transfers.** PostHog EU + Supabase Frankfurt keep most EU data in EU. Paddle, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Sentry, Vercel involve US transfer — disclose SCCs in DPA.
8. **GDPR rights.** Article 15 (access), 16 (rectification), 17 (deletion — see `21-DATA-GOVERNANCE.md`), 20 (portability — `GET /api/account/export`), 21 (objection).
9. **How to exercise rights.** `support@beamixai.com` OR Settings → Privacy panel in-app.
10. **Cookies.** Cross-link to Cookie Policy.
11. **Children.** Service not intended for under-16.
12. **Updates.** Material changes notified via email + in-app banner.

## Cookie Policy + consent banner

**Library:** `react-cookie-consent` (lightweight, react-friendly) OR `vanilla-cookieconsent` (more configurable). Default recommendation: **`react-cookie-consent`** (smaller bundle, easier for Wave 2 Worker 5 to wire).

**Categories:**
- **Necessary** (always on): auth session cookies (Supabase), CSRF tokens, cookie-consent state.
- **Analytics** (opt-in): PostHog `ph_*` cookies — autocapture, session, identify.
- **Marketing** (opt-in, optional for MVP): currently nothing — reserved for retargeting pixels post-launch.

**Banner behavior:**
- First visit → banner appears at bottom with "Accept all / Reject all / Customize".
- Until consent recorded → PostHog SDK is NOT initialized (block at the import boundary).
- Consent stored in `cookieconsent_status` cookie with 1-year expiry.
- Settings → Privacy panel exposes a "Manage cookies" link that re-opens the banner.

**Cookie list (for the Cookie Policy page):**
- `sb-*` (Supabase auth) — necessary, session
- `ph_phc_*` (PostHog distinct_id + session) — analytics, 1 year
- `cookieconsent_status` — necessary, 1 year
- `__cf_bm` (Cloudflare Turnstile bot mgmt) — necessary, 30 min

## Refund policy — explicit copy

Lives at `/legal/terms` §Refunds AND linked from Settings → Billing.

> **14-day money-back guarantee.**
> If you're not satisfied within 14 days of your first subscription charge, request a refund via `support@beamixai.com`. We refund within 5 business days to the original card via Paddle.
>
> **Run-aware refund** (per fair-use policy): if you've consumed **50% or more** of your monthly AI Run allotment by the time you request the refund, the refund is **capped at 50% of the subscription price paid**. If you've consumed less than 50%, you receive a full refund. (Locked per ADQ-5, 2026-05-14.)
>
> **User-initiated runs only count toward the 50% threshold** (clarified 2026-05-16 by board verdict). Credit consumption used for the 50% threshold is **user-initiated runs only**. Runs initiated by Beamix automation (Day-1 onboarding chain, scheduled weekly scans, scheduled freshness checks) do NOT count toward the consumption threshold for refund-cap purposes. This is shown to the customer in their refund-request preview as: `Total runs used: X | Auto-runs (not counted): Y | Counted toward cap: X-Y`.
>
> **Refund policy excerpt + receipt in Hebrew.** T&Cs and full Privacy Policy may remain English at launch (legal review cost). Refund policy excerpt, Paddle receipt (Paddle supports HE), and refund-processed email MUST ship in Hebrew at launch for IL customers (detected via `business.locale = 'he'` or IL phone country code).
>
> **Top-up packs are non-refundable** once any AI Run from the pack has been consumed.
>
> **After 14 days,** cancel at any time via Settings → Billing. Access continues until the end of your paid period. No partial-month refunds.
