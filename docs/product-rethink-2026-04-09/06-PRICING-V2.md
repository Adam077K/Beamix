## Trial / Refund Mechanic *(Updated 2026-05-23 — agency pivot)*

**60-day no-questions money-back guarantee.** Replaces the old 14-day money-back trial.

- Plain refund, no fine print, no phone tree
- One-click cancel in dashboard Settings
- Customer keeps all work product (content, schema, citations) on cancel
- Activation requirement to qualify for refund: discovery call + property connect + first scan = activation
- Domain + business verification at signup; hard ban on re-signups under new emails
- Held-revenue accounting for the first 60 days — revenue not booked until day 61
- First 100 customers = "Founding Member" cap. If refund rate ≥ 25%, tighten next cohort to 30-day mechanic.
- One-per-account refund rule — refund-then-resubscribe = no second money-back window

## Free Scan → Discovery → Subscription Funnel *(Updated 2026-05-23)*

```
Free scan (no email required) ─────────────────────────────────────────
  ├─ Visibility score per engine
  ├─ 3 named opportunities (no blur)
  └─ "Book your 20-minute discovery call" CTA
              │
              ▼
Discovery call (20 min, agent-led)
  ├─ Brand fingerprint captured
  ├─ Approval preferences set
  ├─ Adam reviews fingerprint (through customer #50)
  └─ Tier selected → Paddle checkout
              │
              ▼
Activation (Day 1: discovery + connect + first scan)
  ├─ 60-day money-back clock starts
  ├─ Held-revenue accounting begins
  └─ First deliverables scheduled in week 1
              │
              ▼
Day 61: revenue booked. Customer continues month-to-month.
```

**Free scan economics:** Cost per free scan ~$0.045. With 1–3% conversion to discovery → tier 1 ($499), break-even at very low conversion. Free scans remain a near-zero-risk acquisition channel.
