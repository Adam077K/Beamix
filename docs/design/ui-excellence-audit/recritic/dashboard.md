## Verdict
The polished dashboard meets the Beamix craft bar. It sits beside Profound and Otterly as one
hand — and is arguably more characterful than either, because the verdict sentence + Fraunces
beat give it a point of view their flatter dashboards lack. No AI tells dominate; all four
graded states are designed.

## What's strong
- **One clear TIER-1 focal (M1/M10).** The AI-search-visibility hero owns the fold: elevated card,
  64px-class mono "71", green score ring. FoundingCohort banner correctly recedes to a flat inset
  (TIER-3); the crew rail is TIER-2. Three felt depth tiers, one hero — no uniform-depth tell.
- **4-step type contract is visibly stepped (M2).** Mono figure 71 → 30px display verdict
  ("You're showing up …") → uppercase tracked grey eyebrow ("AI SEARCH VISIBILITY") → 13–15px body.
  Gaps are obvious; nothing evenly-weighted.
- **One Fraunces beat, placed exactly right (M5).** "— *Good* —" italic-serif on the verdict word
  only, inline in a sans sentence. Not in chrome. Exactly one per screen.
- **Earned asymmetry (M3).** Hero `[1fr_360px]` dominant-column + narrow crew rail. No N-equal grid.
- **Violet structure is glanceable (M6).** "Your crew" panel reads as the agent zone at arm's length
  via violet-tint ground + violet dot + top accent. The "Run scan" CTA is blue; violet stays off
  buttons. The blue=you / violet=agents promise is spatial, not a token detail.
- **Numbers are mono (M11).** 71, 0/100, +8 are tabular Geist Mono; prose is Inter.
- **Signature detail present (M4).** The overall-visibility trend polyline with an endpoint node.
- **Designed empty with two-tier recovery (M8).** "Your first scan sets the baseline" titled context
  + specific next step + primary blue pill ("Run your first scan") + quiet secondary link
  ("View past scans"); crew panel "The crew is watching" with a warm violet character glyph; by-engine
  inset placeholder. No bare centered icon.
- **Mobile (M3 fallback).** Clean single-column collapse, hero legible, blue CTA, no horizontal scroll.
